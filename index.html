About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Rhode Island, where I work on a $10M Office of Naval Research program developing resilient, AI-enabled manufacturing and operations systems. I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Syracuse University, where I studied how reinforcement learning can help cyber-manufacturing systems recover autonomously from sensor failures and attacks.

My work spans reinforcement learning, combinatorial optimization, multi-agent coordination, and vendor-agnostic architectures for heterogeneous industrial equipment. I validate it on physical robots, AGVs, conveyors, and machine tools, not in simulation alone.

I believe the goal of intelligent technology is not autonomy for its own sake. Intelligent systems should absorb the operational complexity of coordinating machines, so that people can focus on the problems that need human judgment, creativity, and expertise.
