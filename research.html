From algorithm to hardware

Physical Testbeds

I have designed, built, and deployed algorithms on four physically distinct robotic testbeds. Each one is a place where decisions made by an algorithm have to survive contact with real equipment.

Cyber-manufacturing recovery

4 robots, an AGV, and 3 manufacturing processes representing a cutting process. Used to deploy RL recovery policies after sensor attacks.

Syracuse University

AGV / cobot factory floor

Grid-structured floor where cobots act as AGVs executing BRKGA routing solutions with multi-agent path finding.

University of Rhode Island

Vendor-agnostic material handling

9 robots, 5 conveyors, an ASRS, and AGVs from four vendors, coordinated over a MOSA/ROS2 architecture.

University of Rhode Island

Shipbuilding process automation

CNC machine, laser cutter, robots, and conveyors moving parts through a shipbuilding-style process on the same architecture.

Other Research

RL for power-grid resilience

At the National Renewable Energy Laboratory, I designed an attacker–defender RL framework that discovered failure modes in grid frequency controllers, benchmarking PPO, A2C, DQN, DDPG, and Q-learning across 120 attack scenarios on HPC.

NREL (DOE) · IEEE PESGM 2024

Manufacturing digital twins

Unity-based digital twins of manufacturing systems with ML-Agents (PPO, DQN) for simulating failures and bottlenecks and prototyping control policies before physical deployment.
